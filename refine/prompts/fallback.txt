You are extracting final answer targets from a Chain-of-Thought (CoT).

Goal:
Extract the answer(s) that the author explicitly gives as final in the CoT.

Hard constraints:
- Do NOT solve the problem.
- Do NOT correct the author's answer.
- Do NOT infer a better answer from the question.
- Do NOT use ground truth or any external answer.
- Targets must come from exact text already present in the CoT.
- If the CoT does not clearly state a final answer, return one unknown target with low confidence.

Target types:
- single_answer: one final answer.
- answer_set: a set/list of final answers for one question.
- subquestion_answer: an answer tied to a clearly identified subquestion, such as part (a) or part b.
- unknown: the final answer is unclear or absent.

Evidence:
- evidence must be an exact span copied from the CoT when possible.
- Do not summarize evidence.

Output ONLY valid JSON with this schema:
{
  "targets": [
    {
      "target_id": 0,
      "type": "single_answer | answer_set | subquestion_answer | unknown",
      "description": "string",
      "items": [],
      "subquestion": null,
      "source": "llm_fallback",
      "evidence": "exact text span",
      "confidence": "high | medium | low"
    }
  ],
  "extraction_flags": []
}
